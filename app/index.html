How we check a deal

The verdict. A discount alone does not make a deal. We score every offer on the size of the cut:

  • REAL DEAL (40%+ off) — a deep, genuine cut. Worth buying if you want the item.
  • GOOD DEAL (20–39% off) — a solid saving on a current product.
  • THIN DEAL (under 20% off) — a small cut. Fine if you were buying anyway; not a reason to buy.
